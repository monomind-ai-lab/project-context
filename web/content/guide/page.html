01 start here

Which one are you?

If you are about to change something in a repository, take the first path. If you are deciding what applies across several repositories you do not work in daily, take the second. Each is three steps, and each ends where the reference material begins.

project-context

You build in a repository

The person, or the agent, about to change something in a project that has Project Context installed.

  1. 1 Quick guide Whether it fits your project, a real NOW.md, and the session loop. Fifteen minutes.
  2. 2 Install What it does to your repo before it does it, the dry run, and how to leave.
  3. 3 Operating the loop The day-2 ritual, and the five things worth having written down by day 7.

Then keep the builder's guide open in month two.

project-hub

You oversee several

The person who authors what applies across every repository, and administers the private one that holds it.

  1. 1 Project Hub What a Hub is and is not, what never travels, and whether you need one at all.
  2. 2 Owner's guide Setting one up, then every command with its real flags, gates and refusals.
  3. 3 The three records What your builders are actually keeping, so you can read a pull without guessing.

A Hub is optional. A single repository needs none of it.

Move through it

Arrow keys, Space, PageUp / PageDown, Home and End. Scroll or swipe works too. On a phone a deck becomes a vertical read rather than a slideshow.

Jump anywhere

The Contents item in the menu lists every slide by name, built from the slides themselves so it cannot drift out of step. Your position is remembered if you close the tab and come back.

Take it with you

The menu will save the deck a copy of itself — one file, no assets folder, opening exactly as it does here. There is a light theme in there too. Both decks are English only; command text and file names would not survive translation anyway.
